# Huddy

**Huddy** is a heads-up display (HUD) mod for BalatroIt provides real-time tracking for essential deck statistics, card procs, and hand levels to help you make more informed decisions during your runs.

## Features

### 🔍 Card Previews & Requirements
- **The Fool Preview**: Automatically identifies and displays the last Tarot or Planet card used.
- **Mail-In Rebate Tracker**: Shows the current rank requirement for the Mail-In Rebate joker.
- **The Idol / Ancient Joker**: Displays the currently targeted rank and suit requirements.
- **Observatory Status**: Clear indicator of whether you have the Observatory voucher effect active.

### 🍀 Advanced Lucky Card Tracking
- **Inventory Tracking**: See exactly how many Lucky cards are left in your draw pile versus your total deck size.
- **Live Statistics**: Tracks every time a Lucky card triggers (including retriggers!) and counts successful hits for both **Mult ($)** and **Money ($)**.
  - *Format: `Lucky Hits: [Money]x$ / [Mult]xM ([Total] played)`*

### 🛡️ Seal Inventory
- Dedicated counters for **Red**, **Blue**, **Purple**, and **Gold** seals.
- Displays `[Remaining in Draw Pile] / [Total in Deck]` for each seal type.
- Color-coded text for quick visual identification.

### 🪐 Planet Level Tracker
- Dynamically sorts and displays your **Top 3** highest-level poker hands.
- Highlights your strongest hand levels in orange for easy reference.

### ⚙️ Full Configuration
- Includes a built-in **Config Menu** in the Steamodded Mod List.
- Toggle individual trackers on or off to customize your HUD exactly how you like it.

## Installation

1. Ensure you have [Steamodded](https://github.com/Steamodded/SMODS) (v1.0.0-BETA or higher) and [Lovely](https://github.com/ethanhs/lovely-injector) installed.
2. Download the `Huddy` folder.
3. Place the folder into your Balatro mods directory.
4. Restart Balatro and enjoy!
